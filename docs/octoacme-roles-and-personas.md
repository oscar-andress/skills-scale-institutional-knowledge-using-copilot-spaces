# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers guide team execution, strengthen delivery health, and translate business priorities into actionable engineering plans. They help maintain focus on throughput, quality, and healthy team operations.

### Responsibilities
- Support planning, staffing, and capacity management for engineering work
- Remove blockers and manage dependencies across teams and disciplines
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on sequencing and milestones
- Coach developers on delivery quality, technical trade-offs, and execution discipline
- Monitor team health and escalate risks before they affect delivery

### Goals
- Improve team predictability and sustainable delivery
- Align technical execution with product priorities and business goals
- Reduce delivery friction and increase engineering accountability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering and planning syncs
- Dependency and blocker reviews with team leads and PMs
- Coaching conversations with developers and cross-functional partners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate roadmap priorities into feasible delivery plans
- Coordinates with Project Managers on schedule, risks, and cross-team dependencies
- Supports Developers by removing execution blockers and clarifying technical priorities

---

## QA / Release Managers

### Role Summary
QA and Release Managers ensure that features meet quality standards and are ready for release. They connect engineering delivery with test readiness, release confidence, and operational continuity.

### Responsibilities
- Define and validate acceptance testing, regression coverage, and release readiness criteria
- Coordinate test planning across feature, integration, and release checkpoints
- Partner with Developers on defect triage and remediation prioritization
- Confirm production readiness and support rollback or mitigation planning
- Communicate release status and risk to stakeholders and project leadership

### Goals
- Reduce defect escape and release risk
- Improve confidence in quality gates and deployment readiness
- Support predictable, low-friction releases

### Typical Communication
- QA signoff meetings and release readiness reviews
- Defect status reporting and triage discussions
- Stakeholder updates during pre-release and post-release checks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on defect ownership and test execution
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance and go/no-go criteria
- Works with Project Managers to schedule QA windows, release milestones, and operational readiness

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads help ensure the project meets applicable policy, privacy, and risk requirements while balancing delivery speed and business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Review security, privacy, and compliance risks across project work
- Define required controls, approvals, and review checkpoints
- Partner with engineering and product teams on risk mitigation and remediation
- Ensure project decisions consider regulatory or contractual obligations
- Escalate critical concerns to sponsors or governance stakeholders when needed

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to security and compliance risk
- Build trust through proactive risk management
- Support safe delivery without unnecessary delays

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and design consultations
- Risk and compliance updates for leadership and stakeholders
- Escalation notices for critical issues or policy exceptions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers on trade-offs between feature goals and risk constraints
- Provides guidance to Developers on secure implementation patterns and validation
- Coordinates with Project Managers on governance timelines, dependencies, and escalation paths

---

## Customer Success / Product Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Product Operations Owners connect the project to customer feedback, adoption signals, and service readiness. They help teams understand real-world usage and customer impact before and after release.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer needs, support feedback, and adoption patterns
- Translate operational signals into backlog insights and prioritization discussions
- Support go-to-market readiness, communications, and launch coordination
- Monitor customer impact after release and share feedback to inform iteration
- Help align product delivery with support and customer experience expectations

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Strengthen the feedback loop between delivery and user outcomes
- Support launch readiness and post-release continuity

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and stakeholder briefings
- Launch readiness and support coordination sessions
- Operational reports on usage, incidents, and user sentiment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Shares customer insights with Product Managers to adjust priority and messaging
- Supports Project Managers with launch coordination and stakeholder communications
- Helps Developers understand real-world friction and expected user behaviors

---

## Data / Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners define how value is measured and how teams learn from product usage, quality, and customer behavior. They turn metrics into evidence for prioritization and iteration.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and measurement plans
- Partner with Product Managers on outcome tracking and experimentation
- Validate whether planned changes are delivering expected business or customer value
- Help interpret performance trends, anomalies, and operational signals
- Support post-release learning and continuous improvement planning

### Goals
- Make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions
- Improve the team’s ability to learn from product usage and outcomes
- Ensure metrics are meaningful, measurable, and connected to business goals

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews, reporting meetings, and milestone check-ins
- Performance dashboards and executive summaries
- Analysis of experiments, adoption trends, and quality signals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers translate outcomes into measurable success criteria
- Provides evidence to Project Managers and stakeholders during milestone reviews
- Works with Developers and QA teams to define the signals that matter for quality and adoption
